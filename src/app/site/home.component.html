After installing

  1. Create your Blendy Play account in the app with your email and a password.
  2. Choose a plan and approve the M-Pesa prompt on your phone.
  3. Press play. Your account works on all your screens.

Blendy Play doesn't host films or shows. It's an app for watching content from a third-party platform without the ads, so which titles are available depends on that platform.
